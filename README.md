## 🎭 The 4 Brand Atmospheres & Moods

| Mood / Atmosphere | Primary Domains | Canvas & Text | Accent Color | Visual Emotion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Warm Artisan & Organic** | Bakeries, Cafes, Restaurants, Real Estate, Hotels | `#FAF8F5` / `#1C1917` | Terracotta `#C2410C` or Forest `#15803D` | Tactile, welcoming, warm stone & wood |

## 🏛️ Bespoke Industry Layouts (No Cookie-Cutter Cloning)

* **Real Estate / Residential**: Hero panorama → Architectural concept & materials → Interactive floor plan selector (SVG preview) → Surrounding infrastructure → Mortgage slider calculator → Sales office passport.
* **Gastronomy / Food**: Atmospheric hero → Chef's seasonal special → Tabbed interactive menu (weights, prices, allergens) → Chef's quote & philosophy → Table reservation widget → Map & kitchen hours.
* **SaaS / DevTools**: Product hero with live interactive preview → Segmented audience switcher → Code language sandbox (TS/Python/Go) → Transparent pricing tiers → Quantifiable customer case studies → Candid FAQ.
* **Clinics / Legal**: Trust hero → Specialist credentials & diplomas → Step-by-step treatment roadmap → Transparent fee matrix → State licenses & accreditations → Direct specialist booking.
